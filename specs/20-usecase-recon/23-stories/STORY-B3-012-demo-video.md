# STORY-B3-012 — Demo video

## Story

As the founder, I need a 90-second demo video recorded — narrating one full reconciliation run
end-to-end — to use in the YC application, the Anthropic × AWS accelerator submission, and
customer-discovery calls.

## Acceptance criteria

AC-1: Given the script from `BackOfficePilot_Sprint_Plan.docx` Section 6,
      when the video is recorded,
      then it covers Opening → Plan → Execute → Reconcile → Audit in approximately 90 seconds.

AC-2: Given the recording,
      when reviewed by a non-engineer (e.g. a friend in finance),
      then they understand what BackOfficePilot does and why it matters without further
      explanation.

AC-3: Given the recording,
      when posted on a Loom (private),
      then a link is added to the YC application + accelerator submission + sales deck.

AC-4: Given the recording,
      when an audio engineer or editor reviews,
      then quality is acceptable for investor use (clear audio, captions, no awkward cuts).

## Definition of done

- [ ] Loom link recorded and linked from sales materials.
- [ ] Captions present.
- [ ] One reference-class viewer (someone who would invest) has watched and given feedback.
