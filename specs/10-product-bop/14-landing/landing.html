How it works

Split the brain. One model thinks. One acts. An orchestrator keeps them honest.

Reasoning is expensive, slow, and smart. Execution is cheap, fast, and dumb. Use the right model for each job — and audit every decision.

The reasoner

Claude Opus 4.6

Reads policy, plans the workflow, classifies exceptions, writes audit narrative. Runs on Amazon Bedrock with prompt caching for ~$0.04 per decision.

The orchestrator

Plan → Execute → Reconcile → Commit

A deterministic state machine. Enforces cost ceilings, JSON-schema validation, hash-chained audit logging, and human-in-the-loop escalation paths.

The executor

Amazon Nova Act

Drives browser and desktop UIs on your existing ERPs, banking portals, and lending CRMs. AgentCore Browser as a fallback. $4.75 per agent-hour, production SLA.
